## What to retain and what to correct

1. Retain the dense price bullets, short analytic paragraphs, weekly comparison,
commodity basis distinctions, logistics sections, sign-off and continuation cue.
2. Name clearance versus passage when both appear. The 16.10 reference's wheat
transport volumes sum to 588.658 kt, while its clearance total is 557.648 kt.
Those are different source populations, not a calculation failure.
Corn similarly has 117.421 kt passage versus 120.632 kt clearance.
3. Keep cumulative-average periods visible. UZ 42.9 versus 37.2 kt/day compares
01-14.10 with 01-06.10, not two non-overlapping weeks. The 324.2 versus 336.3
wagon/day comparison has the same scope restriction.
4. Quote settlement separately from morning Last and TBC intraday observations.
Morning Grain Comments 1016 has CZ6 Last 476.25 at 07:00, not the 474.25 Friday
close. TBC 16.10 has soybean oil 66.67 intraday, not the 67.13 CBOT close.
Its BMD 4622 is the 15.10 close, not 16.10.
5. Keep short tons for CBOT soybean meal. Do not infer processing margins from
price changes in seeds and oils alone, or blame logistics for unchanged SSI
without a source supporting that causal claim.

For 09.10, the earlier verified USDA Snapshot supports corn carryout
1.567 -> 1.849 billion bushels (+282 million; +18.0%). If native commentary
conflicts with the table, preserve the conflict in review rather than silently
combining incompatible figures. That older WASDE is background for 16.10,
not a newly released balance report for the second week.
